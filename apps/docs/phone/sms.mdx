## Send a text

Use an active number, a credential with `sms:send`, and sufficient prepaid credit:

```bash
curl --fail-with-body "https://www.papers.bot/v1/phone-numbers/NUMBER_ID/messages" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $PAPERS_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: appointment-reminder-001" \
  --data '{"to":"+12025550100","text":"Your appointment is tomorrow at 10:00."}'
```

Use the recipient's full international number in E.164 format. Replace the example with a recipient who has agreed to receive your messages. Workspace limits, approval requirements, destination support, and opt-outs still apply.

Poll the returned operation for pending or unknown outcomes. Do not resend with a new key to resolve uncertainty. Provider acceptance is separate from delivery; check message status or delivery events.

## Receive messages

Use `sms:read`:

- `GET /v1/phone-numbers/{id}/messages` lists summaries, newest first.
- `GET /v1/sms/{id}` returns the full message.

Subscribe to `sms.received` through [webhooks](/webhooks/overview) to avoid continuous polling. Its `resourceId` is the SMS ID.

## Messaging availability

A rented number does not guarantee delivery to every country or carrier. Messaging depends on account and carrier requirements, destination availability, and current credit. At zero available credit or when a rental expires, messaging can be paused. Funding the workspace can restore eligible numbers.

Long or non-GSM messages can use multiple segments. Charges depend on destination and segment count. See the dashboard for settled charges; pending costs are not final.

MMS is not currently offered as a self-service product. Use email for file attachments.
